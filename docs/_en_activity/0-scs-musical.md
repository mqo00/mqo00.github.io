In *2023 Spring*, I organized and directed **The SCS Musical**, a musical revue by and for graduate students in CMU's School of Computer Science, with 30+ performers and an audience of 200+.

<!--more-->

The show was a night of performances about grad school life, and, as our poster promised, some "emotional DAMAGE support" for fellow SCS grad students. As director (and performer), I brought together performers from across SCS, coordinated rehearsals, and put the whole show on stage. Coordinating a large performance and a big team with all the media tech is fun lol.
