<div class="bio">
<p>I'm Qianou (Christina) Ma, a Ph.D. candidate at Carnegie Mellon University's Human-Computer Interaction Institute, co-advised by <a href="https://hcii.cmu.edu/people/ken-koedinger">Dr. Ken Koedinger</a> and <a href="https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~sherryw/index.html">Dr. Sherry Tongshuang Wu</a>. I work at the intersection of human–AI interaction, generative AI, and the learning sciences.</p>

<p>Large language models are redefining human work, yet people benefit from them unequally because of how they collaborate with AI and how human–AI systems are designed. In my Ph.D., I've been focused on programming, the frontier where LLMs are changing both what it means to program and who gets to do it. My research asks: <b>how could we enable everyone for the AI era?</b> Specifically, I ask:</p>

<ul class="themes">
  <li><b>What to teach:</b> identifying human skills that stand the test of time as models improve, especially communication and evaluation
    <a class="proj-tag" href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3772318.3791283">Not Everyone Wins, CHI'26</a>
    <a class="proj-tag" href="https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-3487/paper3.pdf">pAIr, AIED'23 Workshop</a></li>
  <li><b>How to teach:</b> building LLM-based learning systems that train these higher-order thinking skills through deliberate practice
    <a class="proj-tag" href="https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-64302-6_19">HypoCompass, AIED'24 🏆</a>
    <a class="proj-tag" href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3731756">ROPE, TOCHI'25</a>
    <a class="proj-tag" href="https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29763-1_7">ImaginAItion, AIED'26</a></li>
  <li><b>How to evaluate:</b> designing systematic, scalable assessments of human learning and human–AI collaboration
    <a class="proj-tag" href="https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.70/">SPHERE, ACL Findings'25</a>
    <a class="proj-tag" href="https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-demo.68/">RECAP, ACL Demo'26</a>
    <a class="proj-tag" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11245">Sim2Real Gap, COLM'26</a></li>
</ul>

<p>My work has been published at top venues such as CHI, TOCHI, AIED, ACL, NeurIPS, and COLM, and featured in media such as <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2025/11/12/opinion/ai-coding-computer-science.html?unlocked_article_code=1.0k8.PiUE.Rotivebto9zN&smid=url-share">The New York Times</a>. Tools I built have been used in university classrooms with hundreds of students. The work I led has won the <a href="https://hcii.cmu.edu/news/scs-faculty-receive-google-academic-research-awards">Google Academic Research Award</a> and has been recognized with distinctions including a <b>Best Paper</b>, <b>Honorable Mention</b>, and the <b>Block Center Fellowship on AI &amp; Society</b>. See <a href="#projects">selected projects</a> below and <a href="research">research</a> for the full publication list.</p>
